Pocket TTS in Basque: real-time speech synthesis on a plain CPU

TL;DR I’ve adapted Kyutai’s Pocket TTS model to Basque: Pocket TTS Basque. It runs on an ordinary computer’s CPU, 3.5 times faster than real time, and it can clone voices. Filling a gap Interesting options for Basque speech synthesis have appeared in recent months. I wrote earlier about the Piper TTS and OmniVoice models. But they sit at two extremes: Piper TTS: light and fast, it runs on any device. But its quality is limited, and it can’t clone voices. OmniVoice: excellent quality and voice cloning. But it’s much bigger and heavier. Pocket TTS sits between the two. Hiding personal data in Basque texts

What is GLiNER2? GLiNER2 is a family of models for extracting entities (names, places, dates…) from text. What makes it special is that it has no fixed list of labels: we tell it what we want to find (“person name”, “phone number”, “email”…), and it looks for those in the text.
